The Mirabilis Rafting Company was founded during a time of crisis. During the Illenial Diversion, hundreds of thousands of monsters surging into the physical world made normal travel impossible. Then explorer Aliander Milescienet found a solution.

He found a creature whose intestines literally led to different planes of existence.
