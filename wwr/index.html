Mirabilis Rafting Company

The Mirabilis Rafting Company was founded when men took a terrible situation and turned it into something wonderful. The Great Serpents were thought at first to be the worst of the monsters unleashed upon the human race. Until it was discovered that by the cosmic wonders of their intestines, humans could physically teleport from one way to another. This revolutionized travel, and now instead of taking a plan to cross the atlantic, you take the 23rd great serpent to get to your morning commute from Surrey to Bangkok. We hope that you choose our services and guarantee that you won't regret it!
